KuliahPlanner Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Thank you for using KuliahPlanner ("we", "our", or "the application"). Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, storage, and handling of information when you use our academic planning web application.

1. Local-First Architecture

KuliahPlanner is built with a local-first architecture. By default:

  • All course schedules, academic calendars, tasks, notes, stashes, and semester configurations are stored locally on your device in your web browser's storage (localStorage).
  • We do not operate a remote database storing your personal academic records.
  • Your data remains under your direct control at all times.

2. Google User Data and OAuth Integration

KuliahPlanner offers an optional cloud synchronization feature powered by Google Identity and the Google Drive API. If you choose to sign in with your Google account, here is exactly how your data is handled:

  • Google Identity (Sign-In): We receive basic profile information (your email address, full name, and avatar image URL) to display your account status inside the application.
  • Requested Google Scope: We request access to https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.
  • Limited Scope Usage: This permission grants the application access only to the files that are specifically created or opened by KuliahPlanner. We cannot read, modify, view, or delete any other files or folders in your personal Google Drive.
  • Dedicated Backup File: The application uses this permission solely to create, read, and synchronize a single backup file named kuliahplanner_backup.json in your personal Google Drive.
Google API Services User Data Policy Disclosure:
KuliahPlanner's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, lease, rent, monetize, or transmit your Google user data or academic records to third parties. All API communication occurs directly between your client browser and Google's official API endpoints. No intermediary servers or trackers are involved.

4. Data Retention and Account Disconnection

You maintain complete control over your synchronization and authorization:

  • Log Out: You can disconnect your Google account from KuliahPlanner at any time by clicking the "Logout" button in the application sidebar or settings panel.
  • Revoke Permission: You can revoke KuliahPlanner's authorization at any time directly through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Deleting Local Data: You can delete all locally stored data at any time by resetting the data in the application settings or clearing your web browser's cache and local storage.

5. Security

Because KuliahPlanner runs entirely client-side in your browser, security relies on standard secure HTTPS communication with Google APIs and standard web browser sandboxing. No OAuth access tokens are stored in plain text on remote external servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our features or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have questions or feedback concerning this Privacy Policy, please submit an inquiry or issue via the project repository.
