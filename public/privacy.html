KuliahPlanner Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Thank you for using KuliahPlanner ("we", "our", or "the application"). Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, storage, and handling of information when you use our academic planning web application.

1. Local-First Architecture

KuliahPlanner is built with a local-first architecture. By default:

  • All course schedules, academic calendars, tasks, notes, stashes, and semester configurations are stored locally on your device in your web browser's storage (localStorage).
  • We do not operate a remote database storing your personal academic records.
  • Your data remains under your direct control at all times.

2. Cloud Synchronization and Supabase Integration

KuliahPlanner offers an optional cloud synchronization feature powered by Supabase. If you choose to create an account and sign in, here is exactly how your data is handled:

  • Username & Authentication: We store your username and credentials securely via Supabase Auth. Email verification is not required.
  • Encrypted Academic Records: Your course schedules, tasks, configurations, and notes are synchronized to a private user_backups table.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Access to your cloud data is strictly isolated using PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS). Only your authenticated user ID (auth.uid()) can read, insert, or modify your records.
Data Privacy Guarantee:
KuliahPlanner is built with a local-first philosophy. Your data lives on your device and is only synchronized to your private cloud storage when you choose to connect an account.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, lease, rent, monetize, or transmit your user data or academic records to advertisers or third parties. All API communication occurs directly between your client application and the secure Supabase API endpoints over HTTPS.

4. Data Retention and Account Disconnection

You maintain complete control over your synchronization and account:

  • Log Out: You can disconnect your account from KuliahPlanner at any time by clicking the "Logout" button in the application sidebar or settings panel.
  • Deleting Local Data: You can delete all locally stored data at any time by resetting the data in the application settings or clearing your web browser's cache and local storage.

5. Security

Security is maintained via modern industry standards: TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit, JWT token session storage, and database-level Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring strict multi-tenant isolation.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our features or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have questions or feedback concerning this Privacy Policy, please submit an inquiry or issue via the project repository.
